# PawPath UML Model Results

[Project overview](../README.md)

Seven full diagrams and 12 reading panels are preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied package. Full images are native Enterprise Architect PNG exports. Panels are supplied crops/enlargements with repeated headings for readability; they are not additional UML models.

The full exports retain their original resolution. The enlarged panels provide detailed views of the longer activity, sequence and class diagrams.

The full exports preserve the submitted revision snapshot. Some class connector endpoints and multiplicities are crowded. The enlarged panels and native model provide the detail needed for endpoint review; a zero-warning tool result does not establish complete visual clarity or semantic correctness.
